# Unlocking the Potential of Floating Solar Photovoltaics in Central and Southern America: Deployability and Techno-economic Assessment

Implementation of the paper "Unlocking the Potential of Floating Solar Photovoltaics in Central and Southern America: Deployability and Techno-economic Assessment".

Key ‘Highlights’ for the article titled ‘Unlocking the Potential of Floating Solar Photovoltaics in Central and Southern America: Deployability and Techno-economic Assessment’ are listed below.
1. Among the continents studied, Central and South America holds the highest generation potential, levelized on
the available surface area of the water body.
2. Highly favorable energy yield for FSPVs at major waterbodies in Nicaragua, Honduras, and Guyana
ranges at around 2000 kWh/kW.
3. Special use cases of colocation with AI data center plants and colocation within hydro reservoirs are
discussed.
4. FSPV use case in El Cajon dam illustrates 130.2 tons of CO2, 2.2 tons of NOx, and 2.6 tons of SO2
per MW reduction.
5. Favoring energy policies related to renewable and low-carbon energy sources from Brazil, Argentina,
and Chile is studied.
